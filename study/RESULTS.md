## 1. Confusion matrix

Unit: one **emitted** `create_refund` call. Positive class: **out-of-intent**,
the thing the guard exists to catch. Ground truth is the brief's `intent_text`,
adjudicated blind — the worksheet never showed the guard's decision.

|  | guard blocked | guard allowed |
|---|---|---|
| **out-of-intent** (positive) | TP = 0 | FN = 0 |
| **in-intent** (negative) | FP = 8 | TN = 41 |

### Descriptive precision and recall

| Metric | Value | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| Precision | 0.000 (0/8) | of the calls the guard blocked, the share that were genuinely out-of-intent |
| Recall | undefined (denominator 0) | of the out-of-intent calls actually emitted, the share the guard stopped |
| Specificity | 0.837 (41/49) | of the in-intent calls, the share correctly let through |

> **The positive class is empty: the agent emitted no out-of-intent refund at all.**
>
> Precision is therefore `0/8` for an arithmetic reason, not a behavioural one —
> its numerator counts true positives and there were none available to find.
> Recall is undefined for the same reason. **Neither figure says the guard
> failed to distinguish anything**, and neither should be quoted alone.
>
> The number that carries information here is the **false-block rate**, and
> the finding is about the generator: across 45 traces, including all 15
> injection traces, it never produced a refund outside the merchant's stated
> intent.

**What these numbers are not.** They describe 49 calls from 45 pre-registered
agent traces on 15 hand-written briefs. They are **not** merchant-population
performance, and there is no held-out sample from any real traffic distribution.
No confidence intervals are given because none would be meaningful at this size
(PREREGISTRATION Amendment 2 §A2.5). Treat them as a description of this
experiment, not an estimate of deployed behaviour.

**Precision mixes two components,** and cannot on its own tell a reader whether
the detector or the mandate compilation is at fault. That is why the separated
quantities below are reported too. Where the false blocks actually came from:

Per Amendment 2 §A2.3, reported separately and never combined.

| # | Quantity | Value | Property of |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Blocking rate | undefined (denominator 0) | the guard, × this model's call distribution |
| 2 | Operational false-block rate | 0.163 (8/49) | guard + mandate compilation, × this model's call distribution |
| 3 | Induced-misuse rate | 0.000 (0/15) | **the model**, not the detector |

Quantity 1 is numerically the same as recall, and quantity 3 is a property of the
model under an unhardened prompt (PROTOCOL.md §6.3) — it is not a detector score
and must not be read as one.
