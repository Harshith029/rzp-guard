## The one-paragraph version

Arms A and B were author-written and author-labelled. Arm C removed the author
from case selection and failed, because the model almost never misbehaved. Arm D
tried to recover a metric from a synthetic grid and was retracted when its scorer
turned out to read a label the author had written. **Arm E is the one that
counts**: pre-registered before its corpus existed, no label field in the corpus
at all, ground truth from two people who saw neither the authorization nor the
guard's decision.

It reports recall **0.733** — the guard missed eight of thirty out-of-intent
requests — and says where every miss came from.
